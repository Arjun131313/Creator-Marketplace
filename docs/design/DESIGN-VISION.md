# DESIGN VISION

## Product

This is a two-sided marketplace connecting brands with micro-influencers.

The product has two distinct audiences:

1. Creators
2. Brands

The experience should feel different depending on which audience is using the platform.

---

# CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The platform combines three design directions:

## C — Social / Discovery

The creator experience should feel visual, social and highly browsable.

Think:

- Instagram
- Pinterest
- Depop
- Modern creator platforms

Creators should want to explore campaigns and creators.

The product should prioritise:

- photography
- creator content
- visual discovery
- profiles
- campaigns
- social proof
- personality

Avoid making the creator experience feel like enterprise software.

---

# D — Professional Workspace

The brand experience should feel professional, efficient and trustworthy.

Think:

- Stripe
- Linear
- Notion
- Modern professional marketplaces

Brands need to efficiently:

- discover creators
- create campaigns
- review applicants
- communicate
- manage collaborations
- track campaigns
- manage payments

The brand dashboard can therefore be more structured and data-driven than the creator experience.

---

# E — Gen Z Personality

The entire product should have a distinctive, modern creator-economy personality.

It should feel:

- energetic
- modern
- youthful
- confident
- human
- visual
- slightly playful
- memorable

It should NOT feel:

- corporate
- generic
- boring
- overly technical
- AI-generated
- like a generic SaaS template

---

# OVERALL PRODUCT FEEL

The product should feel like:

SOCIAL + PROFESSIONAL + PERSONAL + PLAYFUL

The interface should feel like a marketplace first and software second.

The product is about PEOPLE and CONTENT.

The UI should therefore not be dominated by:

- dashboards
- statistics
- cards
- containers
- excessive icons

Instead prioritise:

- people
- creator photography
- content
- campaigns
- discovery
- typography
- whitespace
- clear actions

---

# IMPORTANT

Do not redesign the website by simply making the existing components prettier.

Rethink the visual hierarchy.

If a page currently contains ten cards, do not automatically redesign it as ten nicer cards.

Ask:

"Does this information actually need to be contained in a card?"

Use layout, typography, spacing, imagery and subtle borders to create hierarchy.

---

# CREATOR EXPERIENCE

The creator experience should feel closer to a social marketplace.

Creators should be able to:

- discover campaigns
- discover brands
- manage applications
- communicate
- manage their profile
- track collaborations
- view earnings

The primary motivation should be:

"Show me opportunities I might want."

---

# BRAND EXPERIENCE

The brand experience should feel closer to a professional marketplace/workspace.

Brands should be able to:

- discover creators
- filter creators
- create campaigns
- review applicants
- communicate
- manage collaborations
- manage payments

The primary motivation should be:

"Help me find and manage the right creators."

---

# VISUAL PRIORITIES

Priority 1: Creator/content imagery
Priority 2: Typography
Priority 3: Clear hierarchy
Priority 4: Whitespace
Priority 5: Colour
Priority 6: Cards and containers

Cards should NOT be the default way of structuring every piece of information.

---

# WHAT WE ARE AVOIDING

Do NOT use:

- generic AI SaaS layouts
- excessive rounded rectangles
- excessive gradients
- purple AI aesthetics
- glassmorphism
- excessive shadows
- excessive floating cards
- giant meaningless headings
- excessive whitespace
- repetitive dashboard cards
- unnecessary icons
- overly complex navigation
- random animations
- generic stock photography

---

# BRAND PERSONALITY

The product should feel like a company that understands the creator economy.

It should feel confident enough to be distinctive.

Do not make everything perfectly symmetrical or overly sterile.

Allow some personality through:

- typography
- colour
- imagery
- layout
- micro-interactions
- copy
